How service works

Simple from request to delivery.

  1. Request service

    Submit your delivery information online or call our team.

  2. Dispatch

    AE Logistics creates and assigns your delivery.

  3. Pickup

    Your courier verifies and accepts the shipment.

  4. Transport

    Your shipment moves through the AE Logistics delivery workflow.

  5. Delivery

    The recipient accepts the shipment and the delivery is documented.
