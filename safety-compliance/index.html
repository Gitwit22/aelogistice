Safety & compliance

Accountability from pickup through delivery.

Our approach is built on careful handling, documented handoffs, and clear communication.

Our practices

How we handle your shipments.

  • Documented handoffs

    Pickups and deliveries are documented so you know when a shipment changed hands and who received it.

  • Your handling instructions

    Shipments are transported as packaged and labeled by the sender, following the handling and temperature instructions you provide.

  • Secure transport

    Shipments are secured in the vehicle and stay with the courier from pickup until delivery.

  • Clear communication

    Our team keeps you informed and reaches out promptly if a delay or exception occurs.

  • Courier standards

    Couriers are expected to follow AE Logistics procedures for professionalism, safe driving, handling, and documentation.

  • Exception reporting

    Delays, damage, refusals, and other exceptions are reported to our team and communicated to you.

Your requirements, reviewed up front.

Healthcare organizations and businesses often have specific handling, documentation, privacy, or regulatory requirements.

Include them in your service request. We will review them with you before service begins and confirm what we can support, so there are no surprises once shipments are moving.
