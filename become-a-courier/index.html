What we expect

The standard every AE courier meets.

  • Professionalism

    Represent AE Logistics with courtesy and a professional appearance at every stop.

  • Reliability

    Show up on time, prepared, and ready to complete assigned routes.

  • Communication

    Keep dispatch informed and respond promptly to updates and changes.

  • Safe transportation

    Drive safely and secure shipments properly while in transit.

  • Timeliness

    Respect pickup windows and delivery deadlines.

  • Customer service

    Treat every customer, facility, and recipient with respect.

  • Following procedures

    Follow AE Logistics handling, handoff, and documentation procedures every time.
